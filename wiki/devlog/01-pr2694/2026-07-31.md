## Actions Taken

### 1. Julia 1.12 Compatibility Fixes

**a. `runtests.jl:44` — `proc.pid` removed in Julia 1.12**
- Replaced `proc.pid` with `Base.getpid(proc)`, which works on both Julia 1.6 and 1.12.
- Also fixed process-group kill syntax: `kill -INT -$(pgid)` → `kill -INT -- -$(pgid)` (`--` is required to signal the process group rather than the PID).

**b. `synchronized_distributedtest.jl:14` — `@functor struct` broken on Julia 1.12**
- Functors 0.5.2's `@functor struct TraversalModel` syntax fails on Julia 1.12: `MethodError: no method matching fieldnames(::Nothing)`.
- Root cause: the macro expansion chain in `functorm()` → `makefunctor()` receives `Nothing` type instead of the struct type.
- Fix: separate `struct TraversalModel ... end` from `@functor TraversalModel`. The post-definition `@functor` call works correctly.
